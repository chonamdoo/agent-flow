# Product Brief

Use during the full-feature product-brief phase.

Ask YC-style forcing questions before implementation:

1. Demand Reality: what behavior proves people want this?
2. Status Quo: how do they solve it today?
3. Desperate Specificity: who is the most painful target user?
4. Narrowest Wedge: what is the smallest version worth using now?
5. Observation: what concrete user behavior was observed?
6. Future Fit: why is now the right time?
